About Us

Warriors Dream Series is one of India's fastest-growing mixed martial arts promotions. It was built around a single goal: give Indian fighters — from grassroots amateurs to seasoned professionals — a serious, well-run stage to compete on and build a career around.

What started as a response to the lack of structured, high-quality MMA competition in India has grown into a promotion that now runs a full annual calendar of events, spanning amateur development series through to championship-level main cards.

Our Mission

To give Indian fighters a credible, long-term career path through consistent, professionally run competition, structured athlete development, and strong partnerships across the sport.

WDS is working to speed up mainstream adoption of MMA in India — building not just events, but an ecosystem that can compete on the world stage.

Our Story

WDS started from a simple observation: India has no shortage of fighting talent, only a shortage of stages worthy of it. Across small gyms and big cities alike, fighters were training hard with nowhere to prove themselves at a professional level.

Every WDS card is meant to be more than a night of fights — it's a statement that Indian MMA has genuinely arrived, and that the next generation of champions will be forged right here.
